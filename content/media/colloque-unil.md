This talk introduces the concepts of fairness in machine learning, with a focus
on healthcare applications. It was part of the *Rencontre entre la Science et le
Droit dans le Numérique*, a
[colloquium](https://wp.unil.ch/digiweek-fdca/colloque-2022/) at the
[University of Lausanne](https://unil.ch) on the intersection between law
students, researchers and computer scientists.
