The third [MICROCITY Day](https://www.microcity.ch/actualites/microcity-day---19-mars-2025),
held in Neuchâtel on 19 March 2025, featured three innovation ecosystems of the
region: watchmaking materials, hydrogen as an energy vector, and connected health
and health data.

I took part in the third round table, *Diagnostic et santé connectée*, on how
medical diagnosis is being reshaped at the meeting point of artificial
intelligence, biomedical sensors and the life sciences. The discussion sat within
Microcity's [Human Lean Diagnostics](https://hld-ne.ch/) programme, which aims to
reconcile technological innovation with human expertise for faster, more accurate
and more widely accessible diagnosis. The other panellists were Alain Gervaix
(Hôpitaux Universitaires de Genève, founder of Onescope), Jérôme Charmet
(HE-Arc Ingénierie) and Véronique Viette (Fondation ADMED).
