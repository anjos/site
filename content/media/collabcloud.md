The [Loterie Romande](https://loro.ch) published this portrait of **CollabCloud**
among the projects it supports, under *Formation et recherche*. The piece
presents the platform as a way to connect researchers, companies and the public
around artificial intelligence that is useful and inclusive, and the four
domains the [Idiap Research Institute](https://www.idiap.ch) works in:
human–AI collaboration, sustainable societies, AI for the life sciences, and
access to AI.

I speak in the accompanying video about what the platform is for — the shared
computing resources research of this kind needs, and the applications it
carries, from predictive medical diagnosis to assistive robotics. The Loterie
Romande's support paid for CollabCloud's first hardware: state-of-the-art
computing equipment and a Swiss-built assistance robot.
