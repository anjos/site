An intervention at the twentieth edition of the
[Forum des 100](https://www.forumdes100.ch/edition-24), the annual gathering
organised by *Le Temps*, held in Lausanne on 31 October 2024 under the theme
*À quoi sert la Suisse romande?*. The forum brings together around a thousand
figures from Swiss economic, political, scientific and cultural life.

The talk, *Démocratiser l'accès à la puissance de l'IA*, presented a project
supported by the Loterie Romande on widening access to the computing
infrastructure that modern artificial intelligence depends on.
