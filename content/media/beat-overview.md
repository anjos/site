The [BEAT platform](https://www.idiap.ch/software/beat/platform/) is a European
computing e-infrastructure for
[open science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_science), proposing a solution
for open access, scientific information sharing and re-use, including data and
source code, while protecting privacy and confidentiality. It allows easy online
access to experimentation and testing in computational science: you define which
data and modules to use, and the system runs them and returns a result. Data from
different experiments can be easily compared and searched. The platform also
provides an attestation mechanism for reports, whether scientific papers,
technical documents or certifications. I am one of the authors and the architect
of the platform, and I coordinate work on it at the
[Idiap Research Institute](https://www.idiap.ch).

This video is a beginner's tutorial on the platform's main features and
concepts. For more information, visit the platform website or get in touch
through [the mailing list](https://groups.google.com/g/beat-devel).
