This course (EE-612, listed in the [EPFL coursebook](https://edu.epfl.ch/))
presents fundamental tools used in machine learning and pattern recognition,
ranging from the most basic to more advanced (logistic regression, PCA, LDA,
multi-layer perceptrons, deep learning, Gaussian mixture models, and support
vector machines). It is given to post-graduate (Ph.D.) students at the
[École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne](https://www.epfl.ch), Switzerland,
and can serve as a prerequisite for more advanced courses on machine learning.

It ran six times, biennially in odd years, from 2013 to 2023.

**Program**

- **Credits:** 4 {{< ects >}} (100–120 working hours)
- **Grading:** lab assignments (40%) and final exam (60%)
- **Prerequisites:** linear algebra, probability and statistics, Python
- **Topics:** linear and logistic regression, k-nearest neighbours, decision
  trees, boosting, dimensionality reduction (PCA, LDA, t-SNE), probability
  distribution modelling, and neural networks / deep learning.
