The Biometrics Group at the Idiap Research Institute, photographed in the
institute's atrium and offices a few months after I joined in 2010. The group
worked on face and vein biometrics and on presentation-attack detection, the
subject of the [biometrics and presentation-attack detection](/projects/biometrics-pad/)
project.
