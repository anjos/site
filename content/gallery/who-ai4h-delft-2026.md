The pre-convening of the WHO Collaborating Consortium on AI for Health, held on
17 and 18 March 2026 at the Digital Ethics Centre of Delft University of
Technology, a WHO Collaborating Centre on AI for health governance and ethics.
The two-day session brought together fifteen institutes from around the world
around rigorous multi-country evaluation, localisation of AI tools to clinical
workflows, safe dataset sharing across geographies, and turning ethics
principles into measurable frameworks.
