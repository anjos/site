In the ATLAS assembly hall during the visit of the Brazilian Minister of State
for Science and Technology, Ronaldo Mota Sardenberg, holding one of the trigger
electronics boards. On that occasion Brazil and CERN
[expressed interest in establishing closer links](https://home.cern/brazil-and-cern-express-interest-in-establishing-closer-links/):
the Minister and CERN Director-General Luciano Maiani issued a joint statement
supporting the long-term continuation of the cooperation agreement first
established in 1990, and agreed that Brazilian participation in the Large Hadron
Collider should be strengthened.
